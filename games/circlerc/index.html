CircleRC is a top-down time-trial game where you control a small remote control car that can only steer left and right. Circle around objectives to capture them, and get to the flag in the shortest time possible!

I originally made CircleRC for Ludum Dare 47 on the theme "Stuck in a loop". You can find the original Ludum Dare 47 entry page here and you can download the original entry by finding the specific release on itch.io or GitHub.

But you probably want to play the newly-updated fulle game! So follow one of the buttons below to play:
