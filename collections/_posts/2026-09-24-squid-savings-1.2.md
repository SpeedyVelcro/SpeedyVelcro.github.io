I've just released the 1.2 update for [Squid Savings](/games/squid-savings)!
It's more of a technical update, but it's an important one, so read on
for the change notes.

<!--more-->

This update backports my new achievement system - which debuted in
Advanced Invasion - to Squid Savings. The main benefit is that
achievements are now tracked locally, so in the event medals/trophies
fail to unlock on Newgrounds/Game Jolt you can unlock them by hitting
a sync button in-game.

Achievements can also be earned offline and on platforms without
achievement APIs (e.g. itch.io).

## Change Notes
### UI
- Added in-game achievement menu with sync capabilities
- Added achievement popups
- Overhauled about menu

### Technicals
- Upgrade engine to Godot 4.7.2
- Due to physics changes in Godot 4, there may be minor differences to
  the physics in the game (though effort has been made to closely
  match the physics in the previous update).
